orca orchestration ask

Usage: orca orchestration ask (--question <text> | --resume <message_id>) [--to <run:id>] [--run <run_id>] [--options <csv>] [--timeout-ms <n>] [--from <handle>] [--retry-request <id>] [--json]

Ask the coordinator a question and block until answered

Options:
  --help                 Show this help message
  --json                 Emit machine-readable JSON
  --pairing-code
  --environment
  --to
  --run
  --question
  --resume
  --dispatch-capability
  --options
  --timeout-ms <ms>     Maximum wait time before timing out
  --from
  --retry-request

Notes:
  From an active Dispatch, a new question defaults to its owning Run mailbox.
  Timeout leaves the question pending; resume with the original message ID.
